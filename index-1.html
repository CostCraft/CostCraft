💡 How To Use CostCraft

Step 1: Add the cost of the ingredients used in one portion.
Step 2: Add your estimated labor cost for that portion.
Step 3: Add other costs such as packaging or supplies.
Step 4: Enter the menu selling price.
Step 5: Click Calculate Cost.

Tip: Many restaurants aim for a food cost percentage somewhere around 25–35%, but your ideal target depends on your concept, labor costs, overhead, menu mix, and pricing strategy.
